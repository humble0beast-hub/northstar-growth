06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Who is business coaching for?+

Northstar is designed for independent founders, consultants and small business owners who are ready to make more intentional decisions and build sustainable momentum.

Do I need to be an established business?+

Not necessarily. The best starting point is having a real idea or business direction and being willing to take action. We'll identify the support that makes sense for your stage.

What happens on the first call?+

We'll talk about where you are, what you want to change and what's getting in the way. It's a relaxed conversation to see whether the fit is right.

Are sessions available online?+

Yes. Coaching can be delivered remotely, so you can work together from wherever you run your business.
